What actually happens on the day.

First

We pick a place and a look

Studio, your home or a park with good light. We send a short outfit guide so everyone looks put together, not matching.

Then

We let it happen

Light direction for the grown-ups, then we step back. Kids ignore us within ten minutes, which is when the real photos start.

After

You get the gallery

A private link within two weeks. Retouched by hand, full resolution, yours to print and send to grandma.

The ones parents always ask.

Studio or outdoors — which is better for us?

Studio if you want clean, timeless portraits you'll frame. Outdoors or at home if you want the feel of a real afternoon. Most families with kids under six do better at home or in a park.

What should we wear?

We send a guide after booking. Short version: two or three colors that go together, no logos, no everybody-in-white. Bring one change for the kids.

Our toddler won't cooperate. Should we wait?

No. Toddlers never cooperate and the photos are better for it. We plan the session around play, not poses.

Can grandparents join?

Yes, and they should. The Big one package is built for three generations; for Short and Session, just tell us how many people so we can plan the light.
