What actually happens on the day.

First

We get there early

Before the guests. We shoot the room while it's still tidy — the table, the cake, the decorations you spent all week on.

Then

We let it happen

No lining kids up, no "everybody say cheese". Kids ignore us within ten minutes, which is exactly when the good photos start.

After

You get the gallery

A private link within a week. Full resolution, edited by hand, yours to download, print and send to grandma.

The ones parents always ask.

My kid is shy around strangers. Will that be a problem?

No. We don't approach kids or ask them to do anything. We stay at the edge of the room with a long lens for the first while, and by the time the games start most kids have stopped noticing us entirely.

Do you photograph the other children too?

Yes — guests, siblings, cousins, parents. If another family asks for a photo of their own kid, we'll take it. It's your gallery, but nobody goes home unphotographed.

What if the party is indoors and dark?

That's most of them. We shoot with fast lenses made for low light, so we don't need to blast a flash into anyone's face. If a room is genuinely too dark we'll bring a soft light and bounce it off the ceiling.

How many photos do we actually get?

Roughly 40 per hour of coverage, finished and edited. We don't deliver unedited files or pad the count with near-duplicates.

Can we use the photos on social media?

They're yours — post them, print them, put them on the wall. If you'd rather we didn't share any of them anywhere, just tell us and we won't.

What if it rains, or we have to move the date?

Tell us as soon as you know and we'll move it, no fee. If we're already booked on the new date we'll refund the deposit in full.
